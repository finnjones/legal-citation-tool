What the service does

You upload a Word (.docx) document. An AI model reads its footnotes, and our code rewrites each citation to the Australian Guide to Legal Citation (4th ed), adds Ibid and (n x) references and short titles, and can add a bibliography. You get the document back with the edits as Word tracked changes.

It changes the formatting of citations only. It doesn't change your argument, your quotations or which sources you cite.

Checking the result is your job

Every change is shown to you before you download, and anything uncertain is marked Check. Please review the changes, especially the Check items, before you submit your work. Some units ask for a house style that differs from AGLC4; follow your unit's instructions where they differ.
