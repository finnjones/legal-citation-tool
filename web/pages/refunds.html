Refunds

When you're not asked to pay, when you're refunded automatically, and how to ask for a refund.

Last updated {{policy_date}}

Footnotes the AI model couldn't read

Sometimes the AI model fails to read some footnotes. Those footnotes are left exactly as you wrote them, and you can have them read again for free (up to twice per document). If more than a few couldn't be read, you aren't asked to pay until they have been.

Automatic refunds

  • If you pay for a document whose files are no longer available, the payment is refunded automatically.
  • If you pay twice for the same document, the second payment is refunded automatically.

Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with, through Square. They usually appear within a few business days, depending on your bank.

Asking for a refund

If the result is clearly wrong, email {{contact}} within 14 days of paying, with your results link and what went wrong. We'll put it right or refund you in full. "Clearly wrong" means, for example:

  • citations formatted in a way that plainly doesn't follow AGLC4;
  • Ibid, (n x) or short titles that point to the wrong source;
  • a corrected document that won't open, or that has lost text that was in your footnotes;
  • most of the corrections missing or broken.

We don't give refunds for a change of mind, for footnotes the tool marked Check (those are flagged for you to finish), for a small slip in an otherwise sound result, or for points where AGLC4 leaves room for judgement or your unit uses a different style.

Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
