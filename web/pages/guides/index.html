Looking for an AGLC4 footnote generator?

{{brand}} doesn't build citations from a form. You write your footnotes however you like, in Word, and {{brand}} rewrites each one to AGLC4. It adds Ibid, (n x) and short titles across the whole essay and, if you want one, a bibliography, and it shows every change as a Word tracked change.
