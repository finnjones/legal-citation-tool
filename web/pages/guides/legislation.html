How to cite legislation in AGLC4, with examples

Chapter 3 of AGLC4 covers legislative materials. An Act is cited by its title and year in italics, then its jurisdiction in brackets, then a pinpoint.

Acts

The title and the year are italicised together (r 3.1.2). The jurisdiction follows in roman type in round brackets, and a pinpoint comes after it with a space and no comma (r 3.1.4). An "(No 2)" in an Act's title stays inside the italics, before the year.

The pinpoint abbreviations are s, ss, pt, div, sch, cl and the like; see pinpoint references for the rules on ranges and hyphens.

Bills

A Bill is cited like an Act, except that the title and year are not italicised (r 3.2).

Regulations and rules

Delegated legislation is cited the same way as an Act, with its own pinpoint abbreviations, such as reg and r (r 3.4).

Constitutions

The Commonwealth Constitution is cited by name alone, without a year or jurisdiction. A State constitution is cited like any other Act (r 3.6).

Citing an Act again

You can give an Act a short title, and later references then use it with the number of the footnote that defined it (r 1.4.1). {{brand}} cites an Act in full each time unless a short title has been defined for it.

An Act that is cited twice in a row can use Ibid in the same way as any other source. See short titles and subsequent references.

Acts in the text of your essay

{{brand}} also checks the body of your essay, not only the footnotes. An Act's title and year are italicised but never its jurisdiction (r 3.1.2), more than one section is written "ss" with an en dash for a range (r 3.1.4), and a footnote number goes after the punctuation, not before it (r 1.1.2).

Before and after

What {{brand}} doesn't cover

{{brand}} handles Acts, Bills, regulations and rules, and constitutions as above. It doesn't format the rest of chapter 3: explanatory memoranda, legislative history such as "as amended by", gazettes, or quasi-legislative materials such as class orders, rulings and practice directions. Check those against the Guide yourself.
