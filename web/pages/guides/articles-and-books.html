How to cite books and book chapters in AGLC4

In AGLC4, a book is cited as: author, title in italics, then a bracket holding the publisher, the edition if it isn't the first, and the year, then any pinpoint after a space (chapter 6). For example: Eric Barendt, Freedom of Speech (Oxford University Press, 2nd ed, 2005) 163. Journal articles (chapter 5) also start with the author, but their title goes in quotation marks.

Authors

In a footnote, give the author's name with the first name first. Two or three authors are joined with commas and "and"; for more than three, give the first author and "et al" (r 4.1.2). A body that is the author, such as a law reform commission, is named as it is (r 4.1.4). Editors are followed by "(ed)" or "(eds)" (r 4.1.3).

Journal articles

An article citation runs: author, the title in single quotation marks, the year, the volume and issue, the journal's name in italics, the first page, and then any pinpoint.

Here 16 is the volume and (4) the issue, written with no space between them. The pinpoint follows the first page after a comma. For the full rules and more examples, see how to cite a journal article.

Books

A book citation runs: author, the title in italics, then a bracket holding the publisher, the edition if it isn't the first, and the year, then any pinpoint. The pinpoint follows the bracket after a space, with no comma.

Write the edition as an ordinal followed by "ed" (r 6.3.2). Leave out words that mark the publisher as a company, such as Pty, Ltd, Co or Inc, so "Lawbook Co" becomes "Lawbook". If the publisher and the author are the same body, the publisher is left out (r 6.3.1):

Chapters in edited books

A chapter has its own title in quotation marks, then "in", then the editor and the book's title (r 6.6.1).

Citing them again

A later reference to a book or article gives the author's surname, "(n x)" and the pinpoint: MacMillan (n 48) 41. If you cite two works by the same author, the title goes after the surname. See subsequent references.

Before and after

Common questions

Is there a comma before the pinpoint in a book citation?

No. The pinpoint follows the closing bracket after a space: (Hart Publishing, 2010) 9.

How do I write the edition?

As an ordinal and "ed" inside the bracket, before the year: 2nd ed. Leave it out for a first edition (r 6.3.2).

Do I include the place of publication?

No. The bracket holds only the publisher, the edition and the year.

Do I include Pty Ltd or Co in the publisher's name?

No. Leave out words that only mark the publisher as a company, so Lawbook Co becomes Lawbook.
