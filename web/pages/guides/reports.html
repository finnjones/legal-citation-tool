How to cite reports and ALRC reports in AGLC4

In AGLC4, a report is cited as: the author (usually a body such as a law reform commission), the title in italics, then the document type and number and the date in brackets, then any pinpoint (r 7.1). For example: Australian Law Reform Commission, Pathways to Justice: Inquiry into the Incarceration Rate of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples (Report No 133, December 2017) 85.

The pattern

  • Author. The person or body responsible for the report, named as it names itself. A body isn't shortened or inverted. Where a commission or committee issues a report in its own name, as the royal commission below did, it is the author. Some reports name no separate author, such as an ad hoc review published under its own title; then start with the title (r 7.1.1).
  • Title. In italics, including any subtitle after a colon.
  • Document type and number. What the report calls itself (Report, Final Report, Inquiry Report, Discussion Paper, Issues Paper) and its number in the series, if it has one: "Report No 133".
  • Date. The date on the report, usually a month and year.
  • Pinpoint. After the closing bracket, with a space and no comma. A paragraph number goes in square brackets after the page. For a report in several volumes, give the volume first: "vol 1, 4".

Examples

Parliamentary committee reports

A report by a parliamentary committee follows the same pattern, with the committee as the author followed by the parliament it belongs to, such as "Parliament of Australia" or "Parliament of Victoria" (r 7.1.2). Submissions to an inquiry and evidence given at a hearing have their own forms; see Hansard and parliamentary materials.

Common questions

Is the title of a report italic in AGLC4?

Yes. A report's title is italicised, like a book's. Only the titles of parts of something, such as articles, chapters and web pages, go in quotation marks.

Do I abbreviate "Australian Law Reform Commission" to ALRC?

Give the author's name as the report gives it, which for the ALRC is its full name. To keep later references short, define a short title for the report instead.

What if the report has no number?

Leave the number out and give just the document type and the date: "(Report, December 2017)".

Where does a paragraph pinpoint go?

After the page, in square brackets, with no comma between them: "209 [7.111]". If you give only a paragraph, it follows the closing bracket on its own.
