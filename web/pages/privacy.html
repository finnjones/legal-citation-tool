Your document

When you check an essay, the .docx is uploaded to our server so a corrected copy can be made. There is no account, so the document isn't linked to your name.

  • The footnotes are sent to an AI model, which reads them and returns their parts (names, years, report series, pinpoints). The body text of your essay is not sent to the AI model.
  • The body text is checked by our own code on our server (for example, footnote numbers after punctuation). It isn't sent anywhere else.
  • What we keep: your uploaded document, the corrected document, the results shown on the page, the file name, and the date. These are kept so you can come back to your results, pay, and download again.
  • How long: {{retention_hours}} hours. Your uploaded document, the corrected document, the results and the file name are deleted automatically {{retention_hours}} hours after you last upload, check out or pay for that document. After that the results link no longer works and the document can't be downloaded again. A record of any order (amount, status and Square's references) is kept for our accounts.

Anyone with your results link (the page address after you check a document) can see the results for that document, so share it only if you mean to.

The AI model

Payments

Payments are taken on Square's own checkout page. Your card details go to Square and never reach our server. We keep a record of each order: the amount, its status, and Square's payment and order references, so we can confirm payment and give refunds.

Free preview limits

To limit free previews to a few a day, we set one cookie (aglc_anon) holding a random identifier, kept for a year. We also record a one-way hash of your IP address, salted and changed every day, so we can count previews per day without keeping your address in our database.

Our web server's own logs record IP addresses for security and troubleshooting. [TODO: how long server logs are kept]
