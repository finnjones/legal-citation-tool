AGLC4 Citation Tool

Format legal citations in Word documents to the Australian Guide to Legal Citation

Only reads the footnotes. The formatting itself is done by fixed AGLC4 rules.

Output

How it works

  1. An AI model reads each footnote and pulls out its parts: names, years, reports, pinpoints.
  2. Tested AGLC4 rules rebuild every citation, adding Ibid, (n x) references and short titles.
  3. Nothing is dropped or invented. A footnote that can't be fully accounted for is left exactly as you wrote it and marked Refer.
  4. Review, then download your .docx with every edit as a Word tracked change.
